Median uses a simple credit-based model. Pick a plan, get an equal-dollar
amount of credits each month, and spend them on whatever you use — events
from your integrations, AI task generation, or both.

<Tip>
  **One dollar in, one dollar of credits.** Whatever you pay for your plan
  becomes credits in your workspace. Nothing is held back.
</Tip>

## How credits work

Each workspace has a single pool of **credits**. Credits replenish every
month at the start of your billing cycle. Two things draw from the pool:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Events" icon="bolt">
    **$0.007 per event**
    <br />
    Any event ingested from a connected integration (Discord, GitHub,
    Linear, Slack, X).
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI usage" icon="sparkles">
    **Charged at cost**
    <br />
    AI task generation is billed at the underlying model's token price —
    no markup.
  </Card>
</Columns>

### AI model pricing

Median uses Anthropic's Claude models. You're charged the actual
per-token price the model provider charges us.

| Model | Input (per 1M tokens) | Output (per 1M tokens) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Haiku 4.5 | $2.00 | $8.00 |
| Claude Sonnet 4.6 | $5.00 | $20.00 |

<Note>
  Most generations run on Haiku. Sonnet is reserved for harder tasks where
  a stronger model materially improves output quality.
</Note>

### Worked example

A workspace on **Plus** ($10 / month) with $10 in credits:

- 700 integration events at $0.007 → **$4.90**
- 200 Haiku generations averaging ~3K input + 1K output tokens →
  ~200 × ($0.006 + $0.008) = **~$2.80**
- Remaining credits at month end → **~$2.30**

If usage exceeded $10, the difference would be auto-charged at the end of
the cycle (see [Overages](#overages) below).

## Overages

If you use more credits than your plan includes, the overage is **billed
automatically** at the end of the cycle at the same rates ($0.007/event,
AI at cost). No multipliers, no surprise tiers.

### Hard cap

Prefer to stop instead of pay for overage? Toggle **"Hard cap usage at
plan credits"** in [Billing](https://median.sh/app/billing).

When enabled and your credits are exhausted:

- AI task generation pauses
- Integrations stop ingesting new events from Discord, Linear, GitHub,
  Slack, and X
- The task board keeps working — you can still create, update, and
  complete tasks
- Service resumes at the next billing cycle, or immediately on plan upgrade

<Warning>
  Hard cap is the conservative choice for predictable spend. Overages are
  the default because most workspaces prefer never to be paused.
</Warning>

## Trial and cancellation

- **Starter** includes a 7-day free trial. No charge until day 8 — cancel
  anytime to avoid being billed.
- Plans can be upgraded, downgraded, or canceled from the Billing page.
- Upgrades take effect immediately and prorate. Downgrades take effect at
  the end of the current cycle.
- Canceling stops future charges; service runs through the end of the
  paid period.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits roll over?">
    No. Credits reset at the start of each billing cycle. If you regularly
    don't use your full allocation, downgrade — every dollar you save is a
    dollar you still get back as credits on the smaller plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why one credit pool instead of separate buckets for AI and events?">
    A single pool means the dollar you didn't spend on events is available
    for AI, and vice versa. You're never told you've run out of one thing
    while sitting on a surplus of another.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are events counted?">
    One event = one inbound message, comment, issue, PR update, mention,
    or similar item ingested from a connected integration. Events you
    create yourself in the app or CLI are not metered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I see exactly what I've spent?">
    Yes. The [Billing dashboard](https://median.sh/app/billing) shows a
    live progress bar of credits used, a daily consumption chart, and
    per-request breakdowns of AI calls (with token counts and cost) in
    [Logs](https://median.sh/app/logs).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to overage charges if I cancel mid-cycle?">
    You're billed for credits used above your plan limit at the time of
    cancellation, on the same invoice that closes out the cycle.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I bring my own AI keys?">
    Not currently. AI is provisioned through Median so we can guarantee
    rate limits, model availability, and consistent pricing across the
    fleet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
